How to use the admin

A short guide for adding and changing things on the KK Architect website.

← Back to the admin

The 4 steps, every time

  1. Choose what to change in the list on the left (Projects, Products, Pages, Site settings).
  2. Change it and look at the preview on the right.
  3. Click Save. This keeps your work as a draft. The live site does not change yet.
  4. Click Set status → Ready (or open Workflow at the top and drag it to Ready), then click Publish. The website updates in about 2 minutes.
Good to know: nothing is lost. Drafts are saved, and every published change can be undone by a developer.

Add a new project (5 minutes)

  1. Click Projects → New Project.
  2. Fill the first boxes: Project name, Category, Location, Year. Status, area and role are optional.
  3. Cover photo: click Choose an image and upload your best wide photo.
  4. Gallery photos: click Add for each photo, upload it and type a short caption.
  5. Project description: write 1–3 short paragraphs.
  6. Turn Show on homepage ON if you want it featured on the home page.
  7. Save, then Ready, then Publish.
Not ready yet? Turn Hide this project (draft) ON. It is saved but nobody sees it on the website. Turn it OFF when you want it live.

The boxes starting with “Extra:” (floor plans, before/after slider, design story, progress updates, climate report…) are optional. Leave them empty if you do not need them.

Put a project first in the list

Use Position in list. Type 1 to show the project first, 2 second. Leave it empty for automatic order (newest year first).

Show a project on the map

Write the city in Location, for example Vesu, Surat. If the city is not on the map yet, add it in Site settings → Map & cities with its latitude and longitude (right-click the city in Google Maps and click the numbers to copy them).

Update a project that is being built

  1. Open the project.
  2. Scroll to Extra: Progress updates and click Add.
  3. Type the date, a title and a short note. A site photo is optional.
  4. When it is finished, change Status to Completed.

Change your phone, email or Instagram

  1. Open Site settings → Contact, social & numbers.
  2. Change the box. The admin tells you if the format is wrong.
  3. WhatsApp number: digits only, with country code, no + or spaces. Example: 919586853858.
  4. Save, Ready, Publish. The footer, Contact page and the call button all update.

Photo tips

  • Cover photo: wide (landscape) works best.
  • Phone cover: optional tall photo for phones (shape 3:4).
  • Photos are made smaller automatically, but very large files (over 10 MB) upload slowly. Export as JPG at about 2000 pixels wide if you can.
  • Do not show client names, phone numbers or exact addresses in floor plans or photos.

If something goes wrong

  • I cannot log in: use “Login with GitHub” with the account that was given access to the website.
  • My change is not on the site: wait 2–3 minutes and refresh. Check that you clicked Publish (not only Save).
  • A red message appears next to a box: that box needs a value or is in the wrong format. Fix it and save again.
  • I made a mistake: do not worry. Ask your developer to restore the earlier version.
