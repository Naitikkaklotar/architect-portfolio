How to use the admin

A short guide for adding and changing things on the KK Architect website.

← Back to Start

The 3 steps, every time

  1. Choose a job on the Start page, or pick something in the list on the left.
  2. Change it and look at the preview on the right. The checklist tells you what is still missing.
  3. Click the black Publish button. That is the only step. The change appears on the website in about 2 minutes.
Good to know: there is no extra Ready or Workflow step. Publish is enough. If a project is not ready, turn on Hide this project so visitors cannot see it.

Add a new project (5 minutes)

  1. On Start, click Add a new project.
  2. 1 · About the project: name, category, location, year. Status, area and role are optional.
  3. 2 · Photos: upload a wide cover photo, then Add each gallery photo with a short caption. Markers and extra photo options stay hidden until you tap More options for this photo.
  4. 3 · The story: write 1–3 short paragraphs. Turn Show on homepage ON if you want it featured.
  5. 4 · Optional extras: skip this unless you need floor plans, a before/after slider, and so on.
  6. Look at the checklist on the right, then click Publish. Wait about 2 minutes and refresh the website.
Not ready yet? Turn Hide this project ON. It is saved but nobody sees it on the website. Turn it OFF when you want it live.

Put a project first in the list

Use Position in list. Type 1 to show the project first, 2 second. Leave it empty for automatic order (newest year first).

Show a project on the map

Write the area in Location, for example Vesu, Surat, and pick the city in City on the map (a dropdown). If the city is not on the map yet, add it in Contact & settings → Map & cities with its latitude and longitude (right-click the city in Google Maps and click the numbers to copy them).

Add markers on a photo (the clickable dots)

  1. In Gallery photos, open the photo and scroll to Clickable markers on this photo.
  2. Click Add, type a Marker name.
  3. Under Marker position the photo appears. Click on it where the marker should be. Click again to move it.
  4. Optional: write a short note shown when the marker is tapped, or pick a product from the list so visitors can open it.

Update a project that is being built

  1. Open the project.
  2. Scroll to Extra: Progress updates and click Add.
  3. Type the date, a title and a short note. A site photo is optional.
  4. When it is finished, change Status to Completed.

Change your phone, email or Instagram

  1. Open Contact & settings → Contact, social & numbers.
  2. Change the box. The admin tells you if the format is wrong.
  3. WhatsApp number: digits only, with country code, no + or spaces. Example: 919586853858.
  4. Click Publish. The footer, Contact page and the call button all update in about 2 minutes.

Checking before you save

In the project editor the right-hand side has a checklist (✓ and ✗) and the page link (for example /projects/vrindavan-bungalow). Make sure the cover photo, location, at least 3 gallery photos and the description are ticked.

The round badge in the bottom-left corner shows whether the website is updating (orange) or up to date (green).

Photo tips

  • Cover photo: wide (landscape) works best.
  • Phone cover: optional tall photo for phones (shape 3:4).
  • Photos are made smaller automatically, but very large files (over 10 MB) upload slowly. Export as JPG at about 2000 pixels wide if you can.
  • Do not show client names, phone numbers or exact addresses in floor plans or photos.

Undo a mistake

  • Just published, not happy? Open the same item, edit it again, then click Publish.
  • Deleting an entry always asks “Are you sure?” first.
  • Already published? Nothing is ever lost. Open the list of every change, find the change by its date and click it. Send that page link to your developer and they will restore the earlier version in a few minutes.

If something goes wrong

  • I cannot log in: use “Login with GitHub” with the account that was given access to the website.
  • My change is not on the site: wait 2–3 minutes and refresh. Check that the bottom-left badge is green. If a project is hidden, turn Hide this project off and Save again.
  • A red message appears next to a box: that box needs a value or is in the wrong format. Fix it and save again.
  • I made a mistake: do not worry. Ask your developer to restore the earlier version.
