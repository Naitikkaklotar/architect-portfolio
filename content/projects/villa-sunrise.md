A courtyard villa oriented to morning light. Thick masonry walls temper heat, while a shallow pool cools the central court and reflects the sky into the living spaces.

Openings are carefully cut to frame trees and distant ridgelines. Materials stay honest — lime plaster, local stone, and teak — so the building ages with the landscape rather than against it.
